## Engineering Approach

I value technical precision, practical constraints, and systems that can be explained clearly. My strongest work combines a concrete user problem with defensible engineering choices: retrieval pipelines that ground LLM responses, sandboxing that limits untrusted code, financial-data models that avoid duplicate writes, and background systems that remain observable and maintainable.
